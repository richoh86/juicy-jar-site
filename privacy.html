Privacy Policy

Juicy Jar · Last updated 25 July 2026

Juicy Jar does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no network code at all, so nothing can leave your device even in principle.

What the app stores

Juicy Jar keeps a small amount of information on your device so the game works the way you expect:

  • Your best score
  • Your sound and haptics preferences
  • The board of a game in progress, so an interrupted run can be resumed

This is written to the app's own private storage on your device. It is never sent anywhere, it is not readable by other apps, and it is deleted when you delete Juicy Jar.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or registration
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking, and no data shared with third parties
  • No third-party SDKs of any kind
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone
  • No internet connection required or used

Children

Juicy Jar is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either.

Changes

If a future version of the app ever collects data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
