How to play

Drop fruit into the jar with one thumb. Two of the same fruit merge into the next one up the ladder — cherry, strawberry, grape, and so on to the watermelon. Everything you drop stays in the jar. If the pile crosses the dashed line and settles there, the run ends.

Questions

What is the chain bonus?
When a merge drops a new fruit straight into its twin, that second merge is a chain and pays a multiple of the normal score. Building cascades is worth far more than merging as soon as you can.
What happens when two watermelons touch?
They cancel each other out for a large bonus, which frees up space at the bottom of a full jar.
I closed the app mid-game. Did I lose my run?
No. The board is saved when the app goes to the background and restored the next time you open it.
Can I turn off the sound or the vibration?
Yes — the two buttons under the NEXT panel toggle sound and haptics independently, and the setting is remembered.
Does the game need an internet connection?
No. Juicy Jar works entirely offline and has no network code.
Is my score stored anywhere?
Only on your device. See the privacy policy.
