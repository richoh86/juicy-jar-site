One-thumb merge puzzle

Keep going and the fruit keeps growing.

Drop fruit into the jar with one thumb. Matching fruit merges into the next one up — but everything you drop stays, and the jar fills faster than you think. Let the pile cross the line and the run is over.

iPhone · Free · Plays offline

Chains

Chain merges multiply your score.

When a merge drops a new fruit straight into its twin, that second merge is a chain — and it pays a multiple of the normal score. Setting up cascades beats merging greedily.

The line

Everything you drop stays.

Real physics — every fruit rolls, settles and nudges the pile. Nothing disappears on its own, so the jar only ever gets fuller. Let the pile cross the dashed line and settle there, and the run ends.

Your best

No timer. No pressure. Just the next run.

One thumb, and your run is saved if you get interrupted. Undo, bomb and one more life after a game over are three items you only use if you want to, each recharged by watching an ad.

Leaderboard

See where you stand, if you want to.

An optional Game Center leaderboard, for when you want to know how your jar compares. Decline it and nothing about the game changes.

What you get, and what you don’t

No forced ads

No banners, no interstitials. Ads appear only when you tap to watch one.

No sign-up

No account, no profile, nothing to register.

Plays offline

The leaderboard and the item ads are the only optional extras.

Biggest fruit bonus

Two of the biggest fruit touch and cancel out for a jackpot.

Two cherries make a strawberry. Two strawberries make a grape.
