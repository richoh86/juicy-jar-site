Juicy Jar

Two cherries make a strawberry.
Keep going and you might just grow a watermelon.

iPhone · iOS 17 or later · free, no ads

How it plays

Drop fruit into the jar with one thumb. Matching fruit merges into the next one up the ladder — but everything you drop stays, and the jar fills faster than you think. Let the pile cross the line and the run is over.

What makes it interesting

Chains, not luck

When a merge drops a fruit straight into its twin, the chain multiplies your score. Engineering cascades beats merging the moment you can.

Real physics

Every fruit rolls, settles and shoves the pile. A careless drop on the left can cost you the run thirty seconds later.

Nothing in the way

No ads, no accounts, no internet. The app has no network code at all, and it is under four megabytes.

Picks up where you left off

Close the app mid-run and the board is waiting exactly as you left it when you come back.

Sixteen languages

English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, French, German, Russian, Italian, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese, Turkish and Arabic — right-to-left included.
