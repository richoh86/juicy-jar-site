Juicy Jar — demo video

Provided for App Review. Screen recording captured on a physical iPhone.

App
Juicy Jar — a single-player physics merge puzzle
What the recording shows
Launching the app from the Home Screen, then a play session: dropping fruit, matching fruit merging into the next one up, a chain merge applying a score multiplier, and the score rising.
Accounts, purchases, permissions
None. The app has no account, login, in-app purchase, subscription, user-generated content, or permission prompt, so there are no such flows to demonstrate.
Network
None. The app makes no network requests and can be reviewed in Airplane Mode.
